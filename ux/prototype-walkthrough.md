# VyapaarSetu Prototype Walkthrough Guide

This document provides a click-by-click prototype walkthrough specification for exploring the VyapaarSetu Business Operating System UI.

---

## 1. Prototype Click Stream: "Start Mode" (0-to-1 Merchant Onboarding)

1. **Step 1: Gateway Landing**
   - Click **🚀 START A BUSINESS** on the home dashboard screen.
   - *Result*: Triggers the AI Niche & Diagnostic Wizard modal.

2. **Step 2: Niche & Capital Diagnostic Input**
   - Enter `Budget: ₹2,50,000`, `Category: Textiles & Apparel`, `Location: Jaipur RJ`.
   - Click **[ Generate AI Strategic Playbook ]**.
   - *Result*: AI Advisor returns recommended capital split (40% Escrow Inventory, 25% Web Store, 25% Marketing, 10% Reserve).

3. **Step 3: Supplier Matching & Order Placement**
   - Click **[ Browse Matched Suppliers ]**.
   - Select **Jaipur BlockPrints & Co** (Composite Score: 96.5, Escrow Verified ✅).
   - Enter initial batch size: `50 units @ ₹450 = ₹22,500`.
   - Click **[ Lock Funds in VyapaarSetu Escrow ]**.

4. **Step 4: Escrow Multi-Sig Hold Confirmation**
   - *Result*: Status changes to `Escrow Secured (Txn ID: ESC-3001)`. Merchant views live tracking badge until delivery confirmation.

## 2. Prototype Click Stream: "Sell Mode" (Turnkey Store Acquisition)

1. **Step 1: Store Exchange Navigation**
   - Click **🏷️ SELL YOUR STORE** or **Store Marketplace** tab.

2. **Step 2: P&L Verification Inspection**
   - Click on listing **STR-502: Vedic Botanicals D2C**.
   - Click **[ View Bank & Gateway Verified Audit Trail ]**.
   - *Result*: Modal opens displaying 12-month verified Shopify revenue, POS counter transactions, and net 21.2% profit margin.

3. **Step 3: Acquisition Deposit**
   - Click **[ Deposit Asking Price into Escrow (₹55,00,000) ]**.
   - *Result*: Locks funds in acquisition escrow and initiates 7-day physical audit window for inventory inspection.
