# VyapaarSetu — End-to-End User Journey Maps

**Target Version:** Version 2.0 (Production Portfolio Edition)  
**Purpose:** Maps step-by-step merchant emotional states, touchpoints, pain points, and delight triggers across platform workflows.

---

## 1. Merchant Journey Map: Store Onboarding to First Escrow Release

```
[Awareness] ➔ [Registration] ➔ [Catalog Import] ➔ [First B2B Order] ➔ [Escrow Release]
```

### Stage 1: Awareness & Discovery
- **Action:** Merchant hears about VyapaarSetu via trade expo or digital ad.
- **Thought:** "Can I really digitize my Surat retail store without losing margins?"
- **Touchpoint:** Landing page hero section & ROI calculator.
- **Emotion:** Neutral / Cautious Optimism.

### Stage 2: Registration & KYC Verification
- **Action:** Merchant registers on `/start-business` and inputs GSTIN.
- **Touchpoint:** 4-step onboarding wizard.
- **Delight Trigger:** Instant auto-validation of GSTIN in under 2 seconds.
- **Emotion:** Impressed / Reassured.

### Stage 3: Catalog Import & Inventory Sync
- **Action:** Uploads existing product inventory CSV or connects Tally ERP.
- **Touchpoint:** `/run-business` inventory grid.
- **Delight Trigger:** Dynamic safety stock & ABC inventory classification badge appears automatically.
- **Emotion:** Empowered.

### Stage 4: Receiving First B2B Order with Escrow
- **Action:** Buyer places ₹85,000 bulk order.
- **Touchpoint:** WhatsApp alert notification + Escrow status badge (`Funded in Vault`).
- **Pain Point Mitigated:** Zero risk of non-payment before shipping.
- **Emotion:** Highly Confident.

### Stage 5: Fulfillment & Escrow Fund Release
- **Action:** Order dispatched via BlueDart; buyer inspects goods and approves release.
- **Touchpoint:** Payout deposit alert into merchant bank account.
- **Delight Trigger:** 100% funds released in 24 hours post-inspection.
- **Emotion:** Loyal Brand Advocate.
