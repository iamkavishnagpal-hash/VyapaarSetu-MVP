# VyapaarSetu — User Personas & Target Archetypes

**Target Version:** Version 2.0 (Production Portfolio Edition)  
**Purpose:** Defines the core user archetypes interacting with the VyapaarSetu platform to ground product UX decisions in empirical merchant needs.

---

## Persona 1: Rajesh Sharma — Tier 2 MSME Retail Merchant
- **Role:** Owner, Sharma Electronics & Hardware (Surat, Gujarat)
- **Demographics:** Age 42 | B.Com Graduate | 15 Years in Offline Wholesale
- **Goals:**
  - Transition offline store sales to digital channels without incurring excessive commission fees.
  - Eliminate working capital lockup caused by slow-moving stock.
  - Source inventory directly from top manufacturers with guaranteed delivery SLAs.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Cash flow friction from delayed customer payments and credit defaults.
  - Frequent stockouts of high-demand items during festival seasons.
  - Lack of technical staff to manage complex ERP systems.
- **Platform Usage:** Daily mobile and desktop access to `/dashboard`, `/run-business`, and `/vendors`.

---

## Persona 2: Ananya Roy — D2C E-Commerce Brand Founder
- **Role:** Founder & CEO, CraftRoot Apparel (Bengaluru, Karnataka)
- **Demographics:** Age 29 | MBA | 4 Years in D2C E-Commerce
- **Goals:**
  - Scale monthly store revenue from ₹500K to ₹2.5M.
  - Acquire verified B2B wholesale buyers across North and West India.
  - Eventually exit/sell store on VyapaarSetu M&A Marketplace for an attractive ARR multiple.
- **Pain Points:**
  - High customer acquisition cost (CAC) on Meta and Google ad channels.
  - Fear of non-payment when selling wholesale to distant buyers.
- **Platform Usage:** Weekly access to `/grow-store`, `/analytics`, and `/sell-store`.

---

## Persona 3: Vikramaditya Singh — B2B Wholesale Distributor
- **Role:** Managing Director, National Industrial Supply Co. (Mumbai, Maharashtra)
- **Demographics:** Age 48 | Supply Chain Executive
- **Goals:**
  - Fulfill large bulk orders with automated buyer escrow payment protection.
  - Maintain 95%+ vendor SLA compliance to retain Tier-1 preferred vendor status.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Manual paper-based RFQs and invoice reconciliation.
  - Logistics delays leading to order cancellations.
- **Platform Usage:** Direct access to `/vendors`, `/run-business` (Order Routing), and Escrow Vault API.

---

## Persona 4: Priya Mehta — Institutional M&A Store Investor
- **Role:** Investment Principal, Commerce Ventures (Mumbai)
- **Demographics:** Age 35 | Private Equity Analyst
- **Goals:**
  - Identify high-health MSME retail storefronts for roll-up acquisitions.
  - Rely on transparent, audited EBITDA and revenue multiples.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Inaccurate offline financial books and unverified revenue claims.
- **Platform Usage:** Monthly review of `/sell-store` listings and audited `business_health.csv` scorecards.
