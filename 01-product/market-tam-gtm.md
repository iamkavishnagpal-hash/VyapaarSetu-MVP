## 2. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Flywheel Acceleration

1. **Phase 1: B2B Industrial Hub Vendor Onboarding (Supply Side)**
   - Partner with textile, brasscraft, and footwear trade associations in Jaipur, Surat, Tirupur, Kanpur, and Moradabad.
   - Offer zero-commission vendor catalog listings in exchange for Escrow Wallet compliance.

2. **Phase 2: D2C & Retail Merchant Acquisition (Demand Side)**
   - Distribute the free **AI Niche & Capital Diagnostic Wizard** to aspiring entrepreneurs.
   - Convert founders into active merchants through 0-to-1 turnkey store launch playbooks.

3. **Phase 3: Turnkey Marketplace & Escrow Financial Monetization**
   - Charge a 1.2% escrow take-rate on vendor POs and a 2.5% success commission on store acquisition transactions.
