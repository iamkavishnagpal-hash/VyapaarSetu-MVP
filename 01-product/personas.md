# VyapaarSetu User Personas

This document details the core target user profiles across the four business operational modes: **Start**, **Run**, **Sell**, and **Grow**.

---

## 1. Persona 1: Ananya — The Aspiring D2C Founder (Start Mode)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ANANYA | Age: 26 | Location: Jaipur, RJ | Role: Founder, AuraCraft Ethnic   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ "I have design ideas for Jaipur block-printed apparel, but zero knowledge   │
│ of sourcing reliable vendors, setting up POS stock, or protecting capital." │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Background**: Ex-marketing designer wanting to launch an ethnic apparel brand.
- **Key Goals**: Start store from zero capital leakage; source verified textiles suppliers with low Min Order Quantities (MOQ).
- **Pain Points**: Fear of being scammed by unverified suppliers; confusion over online storefront vs. physical store counter inventory sync.
- **VyapaarSetu Solution**: AI Start Wizard + Escrow Wallet protection on first vendor orders.
