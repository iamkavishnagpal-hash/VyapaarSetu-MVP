## 2. KPI Governance Matrix (19 Core Metrics)

### 1. Revenue (Gross Platform GMV)
- **Definition:** Total gross monetary value of all completed merchant orders across channels.
- **Formula:** `SUM(quantity * unit_price_inr) WHERE status = 'Delivered'`
- **Business Importance:** Top-line measure of platform traction and economic volume.
- **Recommended Action:** If MoM growth drops below 5%, launch merchant acquisition campaigns.
- **Visual Type:** Area Chart / KPI Card with Sparkline
- **Owner:** Chief Commercial Officer (CCO)
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 2. Profit (Net Platform Earnings)
- **Definition:** Platform commission and merchant fee revenue minus operational fulfillment expenses.
- **Formula:** `Gross Platform Revenue - Total COGS - Shipping Subsidies - Payment Gate Fees`
- **Business Importance:** Ensures business model sustainability and unit economic positivity.
- **Recommended Action:** If net margin drops below 8%, audit shipping subsidies and partner discounts.
- **Visual Type:** Stacked Bar Chart (Revenue vs COGS vs Net Profit)
- **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 3. Gross Margin %
- **Definition:** Percentage of top-line revenue retained after accounting for direct cost of goods sold.
- **Formula:** `((Total Revenue - Total COGS) / Total Revenue) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Direct measure of product category pricing power and supplier negotiation.
- **Recommended Action:** If SKU margin < 15%, evaluate vendor tier re-negotiation or markdown.
- **Visual Type:** Line Chart with Target Threshold Line (25%)
- **Owner:** Merchandising Director
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 4. Net Margin %
- **Definition:** Bottom-line profit percentage remaining after all operational expenses and overhead.
- **Formula:** `(Net Platform Earnings / Total Gross Revenue) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Key profitability metric evaluated by institutional investors.
- **Recommended Action:** If Net Margin < 5%, freeze non-essential marketing spend.
- **Visual Type:** Gauge Chart with Benchmark Bands
- **Owner:** CFO
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 5. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Definition:** Total marketing and sales expense divided by new customer registrations in a given period.
- **Formula:** `Total Marketing Campaign Spend / New Verified Customers Acquired`
- **Business Importance:** Indicates efficiency of growth engines and channel paid acquisition.
- **Recommended Action:** If CAC increases by > 20%, reallocate ad budget to organic/referral channels.
- **Visual Type:** Clustered Column Chart by Acquisition Channel
- **Owner:** VP of Marketing
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 6. Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **Definition:** Projected total revenue generated by a merchant/customer over their active tenure.
- **Formula:** `Average Order Value * Order Frequency per Year * Average Customer Lifespan (Years)`
- **Business Importance:** Foundation for setting sustainable CAC ceilings and customer success investment.
- **Recommended Action:** Aim for LTV/CAC ratio >= 3.0x. If < 2.0x, improve onboarding retention.
- **Visual Type:** Waterfall Chart by Customer Segment
- **Owner:** Head of Customer Success
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 7. Conversion Rate %
- **Definition:** Percentage of unique storefront visitors who complete a paid order.
- **Formula:** `(Total Completed Orders / Total Storefront Visits) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Key UX efficiency indicator evaluating checkout friction and trust signals.
- **Recommended Action:** If conversion rate drops < 2.5%, run A/B test on checkout payment steps.
- **Visual Type:** Funnel Chart (Visits ➔ Cart Add ➔ Checkout ➔ Payment ➔ Order)
- **Owner:** Lead UX Designer / Product Manager
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 8. Retention Rate % (90-Day Cohort)
- **Definition:** Percentage of customers who place at least one order 90 days after registration.
- **Formula:** `(Customers Active in Month 3 / Customers Onboarded in Month 1) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Reflects store stickiness and product-market fit.
- **Recommended Action:** If 90-day retention < 30%, trigger automated lifecycle re-engagement emails.
- **Visual Type:** Cohort Heatmap Matrix
- **Owner:** Head of Growth
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 9. Repeat Purchase Rate %
- **Definition:** Proportion of buyers who have placed more than one order during their lifetime.
- **Formula:** `(Customers with >1 Order / Total Unique Customers) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Indicates customer loyalty and catalog breadth satisfaction.
- **Recommended Action:** Offer personalized cross-sell recommendations via `recommendation_engine.py`.
- **Visual Type:** Donut Chart (Single Buyers vs Repeat Buyers)
- **Owner:** CRM Manager
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 10. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition:** Average gross monetary spend per customer transaction.
- **Formula:** `Total Delivered Order Revenue / Total Number of Orders`
- **Business Importance:** Driver of transaction economics and shipping cost amortization.
- **Recommended Action:** Introduce threshold-based free shipping (e.g., Free Shipping over ₹2,500).
- **Visual Type:** Trend Line with Moving Average
- **Owner:** Commercial Lead
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 11. Order Fulfilment Rate %
- **Definition:** Percentage of total orders processed, dispatched, and delivered within SLA.
- **Formula:** `(On-Time Delivered Orders / Total Placed Orders) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Primary operational SLA driving buyer satisfaction and low dispute rates.
- **Recommended Action:** If fulfillment rate < 92%, re-route pending orders using `order_routing.py`.
- **Visual Type:** Bullet Chart (Actual vs SLA Target 95%)
- **Owner:** Operations Director
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 12. Vendor SLA Compliance %
- **Definition:** Vendor performance score based on lead times, order fill rate, and quality compliance.
- **Formula:** `AVG(quality_score * 0.4 + delivery_score * 0.3 + fulfillment_rate * 0.3)`
- **Business Importance:** Protects platform brand equity and stock availability.
- **Recommended Action:** If vendor SLA < 80%, place supplier on probation status.
- **Visual Type:** Vendor Leaderboard Table / Horizontal Bar Chart
- **Owner:** Vendor Operations Lead
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 13. Inventory Turnover Ratio
- **Definition:** Number of times inventory is sold and replaced over a 12-month period.
- **Formula:** `Annual COGS / Average Inventory Value`
- **Business Importance:** Measures capital efficiency and stock velocity.
- **Recommended Action:** Target 6.0x–8.0x annual turnover. If < 4.0x, initiate clearance sales.
- **Visual Type:** Bar Chart by Product Category
- **Owner:** Supply Chain Analyst
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 14. Dead Stock Ratio %
- **Definition:** Share of warehouse inventory value that has remained unsold for over 90 days.
- **Formula:** `(Value of Inventory > 90 Days Old / Total Inventory Value) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Highlights locked capital and impending obsolescence write-downs.
- **Recommended Action:** If dead stock > 10%, execute liquidation via `pricing_optimizer.py`.
- **Visual Type:** Warning Badge Card & Pie Chart
- **Owner:** Warehouse Manager
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 15. Escrow Completion Rate %
- **Definition:** Percentage of funded escrow transactions successfully released without dispute.
- **Formula:** `(Released Escrow Count / Total Funded Escrow Count) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Demonstrates transaction safety and B2B buyer-seller trust.
- **Recommended Action:** If dispute rate > 3%, audit inspection window policies.
- **Visual Type:** Semi-Circle Donut Gauge
- **Owner:** Risk & Compliance Lead
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 16. Store Health Score
- **Definition:** Composite 0–100 index rating store solvency, order velocity, and customer rating.
- **Formula:** `(Solvency Ratio * 25) + (Fulfillment Rate * 0.35) + (Avg Rating * 8) + (Growth Index * 0.25)`
- **Business Importance:** Benchmarks storefront performance for platform financing or exit.
- **Recommended Action:** If score < 70, assign dedicated merchant success advisor.
- **Visual Type:** Heatmap Color Card Grid
- **Owner:** Merchant Success Lead
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 17. Growth Score Index
- **Definition:** Normalized 0–150 score tracking MoM expansion in active buyers and gross sales.
- **Formula:** `((Current Month GMV / Base Month GMV) * 0.6 + (Current Active Buyers / Base Buyers) * 0.4) * 100`
- **Business Importance:** Core indicator for ecosystem expansion rate.
- **Recommended Action:** Benchmark target >= 120.0.
- **Visual Type:** Sparkline Card with Percentage Change Badge
- **Owner:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)

### 18. Business Risk Score
- **Definition:** 0–100 threat assessment metric aggregating returns, disputes, and delayed payouts.
- **Formula:** `(Return Rate % * 3) + (Dispute Rate % * 5) + (Late Shipping % * 2)`
- **Business Importance:** Early warning signal to prevent financial fraud or store insolvency.
- **Recommended Action:** If Risk Score > 35, freeze automated escrow releases.
- **Visual Type:** Status Pill Indicator (Green / Yellow / Red Alert)
- **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer (CRO)
- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Priority:** P0 (Critical)

### 19. Product Health Score
- **Definition:** 0–100 rating combining customer star ratings, return rate, and stock turnover speed.
- **Formula:** `(Avg Review Stars / 5.0 * 40) + ((100 - Return Rate %) * 0.4) + (Turnover Score * 0.2)`
- **Business Importance:** Curates search algorithm rankings and marketplace placement.
- **Recommended Action:** If Product Health < 60, remove item from featured marketplace carousels.
- **Visual Type:** Star Rating Breakdown & Trend Chart
- **Owner:** Catalog Quality Lead
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Priority:** P1 (High)
