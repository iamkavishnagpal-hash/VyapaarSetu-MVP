# VyapaarSetu Risk Mitigation & 3-Year Scale Playbook

## 1. Risk Mitigation Matrix

| Operational Risk | Mitigation Strategy | System Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulatory / RBI Escrow Compliance** | Partner with RBI-authorized nodal banking partners | Multi-sig compliance smart escrow contracts |
| **Inventory Shrinkage Fraud** | Automated POS billing scan audit vs shelf balance | Dynamic shrinkage alert notifications (≥ 3.0%) |
| **Supplier Defect Spikes** | Automated Tier downgrading for suppliers with defect > 3.0% | Tier 3 mandatory 100% batch QA audit hold |

---

## 2. 3-Year Scale Milestones

- **Year 1**: Onboard 15,000 merchants across 10 tier-1 commerce hubs; process ₹1.2B in escrow GMV.
- **Year 2**: Expand to 60,000 active stores; integrate cross-border B2B escrow for export MSMEs.
- **Year 3**: Onboard 150,000 active merchants; launch AI autonomous purchasing agents.
