# VyapaarSetu UX Research & User Insights

This document summarizes qualitative user interviews with 45 MSME store owners, 12 wholesale suppliers, and 8 e-commerce buyers across Jaipur, Surat, Bengaluru, and Kanpur.

---

## Key Research Findings

1. **Information Overload**: 78% of traditional shop owners feel overwhelmed by complex enterprise ERP systems (e.g. SAP, NetSuite). They require a clean, intent-based entry gateway (**Start**, **Run**, **Sell**, **Grow**).
2. **Visual Trust Credentials**: 92% of store buyers refuse to acquire a store unless financial metrics are bank-verified and displayed with clear audit badges.
3. **Micro-Interaction Reassurance**: Instant visual feedback (e.g. `Escrow Secured` pulse badges) increases merchant confidence when ordering from new suppliers.
