# VyapaarSetu Escrow Wallet Rules & Dispute Resolution Engine

## Multi-Sig Escrow Lock Rules

1. **Deposit Phase**: Buyer or merchant locks 100% of order value into VyapaarSetu Multi-Sig Escrow Wallet.
2. **Hold Phase**: Funds remain locked until carrier confirms delivery or 7-day physical audit window completes.
3. **Milestone Auto-Release**: Payout is automatically released to supplier/seller upon receipt of valid delivery API webhook + zero customer dispute filed within 48 hours.
4. **Dispute Resolution**: In case of fabric defect or missing stock, funds remain frozen in escrow while a VyapaarSetu Platform Senior Auditor verifies physical unboxing video logs.
