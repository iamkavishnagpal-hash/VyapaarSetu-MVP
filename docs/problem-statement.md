# VyapaarSetu — Comprehensive Problem Statement & Industry Context

**Target Version:** Version 2.0 (Production Portfolio Edition)  
**Domain:** MSME Retail Commerce, Supply Chain Logistics, Trade Credit Escrow

---

## 1. Industry Context & Market Opportunity

India’s retail ecosystem comprises over 60 million MSME kiranas, electronics dealers, apparel retailers, and hardware distributors, contributing ~30% of the nation's GDP. Despite rapid consumer-facing digital payment adoption (UPI), B2B supply chain operations for retail merchants remain severely fragmented.

---

## 2. Core Problem Statements

### Problem 1: Intuition-Based Inventory Purchasing & Dead Stock Capital Lockup
- **Symptom:** Small retailers lack automated demand forecasting tools, relying on manual guesswork when reordering stock.
- **Impact:** Over 22% of merchant working capital remains trapped in slow-moving or obsolete inventory (>90 days old), while fast-moving SKUs suffer frequent stockouts during peak demand periods.
- **Quantified Loss:** Average ₹1.4M annual capital lockup per retail store.

### Problem 2: Supplier SLA Defaults & Delivery Latency
- **Symptom:** Merchants source products from unverified local wholesalers with unpredictable lead times, non-standardized defect rates, and fluctuating prices.
- **Impact:** Order fulfillment delays average 4.2 days, leading to customer cancellations and a 15% annual customer churn rate.

### Problem 3: B2B Payment Risk & Lack of Trade Escrow
- **Symptom:** Distant B2B trade requires either 100% upfront payment (high buyer risk) or informal credit terms (high seller default risk).
- **Impact:** Small merchants miss out on lucrative wholesale orders outside their local city due to fear of non-payment or fraudulent returns.

### Problem 4: Opaque Store Valuation for M&A / Exit
- **Symptom:** Successful merchants wishing to sell their retail business lack audited financial records or standard EBITDA valuation multiples.
- **Impact:** M&A transactions take 6–12 months to close, often collapsing due to lack of verified revenue trust.
